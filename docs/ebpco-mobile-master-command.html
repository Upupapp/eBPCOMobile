0   Reading this document

Scope discipline. This document specifies the applicant-facing app only. Evaluation, assessment, approval, and release are admin-side acts. The mobile app never performs them — it reports them, explains them, and collects what the applicant owes in response. Any command that appears to let an applicant advance their own application is a defect.

1   The Master Command

Standing context. Every tab command in Sections 6–10 inherits this and must not contradict it.

Master Command

Build the applicant-facing frontend of eBPCO Mobile as the single place a Philippine property owner, business owner, or their authorised representative can prepare, file, pay for, track, and receive construction-related permits and the Certificate of Occupancy from their LGU's Office of the Building Official — without visiting the City Hall except where physical presence is legally unavoidable.

Every screen must answer three questions without the user asking: where is my application right now, what do I owe or must do next, and when will this be done. A screen that cannot answer all three for its subject is not finished.

1.1   Who this is for

The primary user is not a design professional. They are an owner who has engaged an architect or engineer and who now has to deal with the LGU. They are on a mid-range Android phone, frequently on mobile data, and they are anxious about delay and about being asked for money outside the official schedule. Two secondary users share the same app: the authorised representative acting under a notarised Special Power of Attorney, and the design professional tagged to an application whose PRC and PTR credentials must be captured and kept current.

1.2   Non-negotiables

PrincipleWhat it forces in the UI
Pledged dates, not vague statesEvery in-flight application shows a target completion date derived from its RA 11032 classification (3, 7, or 20 working days), counted in working days, with the day count remaining. "Under Review" alone is a failure.
Deficiency is the critical pathA Letter of Instruction or a Revision Required result is the single most important thing the app can surface. It preempts everything else: top of Home, badge on the tab, push notification, and a persistent banner until resolved.
Money is never ambiguousFees appear only from an official Order of Payment, itemised, with the total in pesos and centavos. The app never estimates an amount that the LGU has not assessed. Never present a payment channel that is not officially accepted.
Zero-contact by defaultWhere RA 11032's zero-contact policy applies, the app must not invite the user to "follow up in person". Every follow-up path is in-app, and any required physical step is stated explicitly with its reason.
Offline-tolerantDrafts, downloaded permits, and the document vault are readable with no connection. Only submission, payment, and refresh require network.
Filipino-legiblePlain language over bureaucratic phrasing. Where a legal term is unavoidable (locational clearance, FSEC, FSIC, Order of Payment), it is shown with a one-line plain explanation on first use.

2   Philippine regulatory baseline

Each instrument below constrains the frontend. The right-hand column is the binding UI consequence — these are requirements, not background reading.

InstrumentWhat it establishesBinding consequence for eBPCO Mobile
PD 1096
National Building Code, and its 2004 Revised IRR		 Building permits are issued by the Building Official on satisfactory plans and a duly notarised application. Ancillary and accessory permits sit under the building permit. A permit expires and becomes null and void if the work is not commenced within one year of issue, or if work is suspended or abandoned. The permit record must carry an issue date and drive a commencement countdown. The app warns at 60 and 30 days before the one-year lapse and states the consequence plainly. Applications must distinguish ancillary/accessory permits from the parent building permit and link them.
RA 11032
Ease of Doing Business and Efficient Government Service Delivery Act of 2018		 Prescribed maximum processing times of 3 working days (simple), 7 (complex), and 20 (highly technical). Zero-contact policy. Mandatory Citizen's Charter. Automatic approval where an agency fails to act within the prescribed period. Business One-Stop Shop. Every application is stamped with its classification and a working-day countdown to the pledged date. The app surfaces the LGU's Citizen's Charter entry for the service. It shows an overdue state when the pledge lapses, and tells the applicant what recourse exists rather than silently continuing to show "Under Review".
Amended JMC 2021-01
ARTA–DPWH–DILG–DICT–DTI–PRC–BFP, amending JMC 2018-01		 Service standards for construction-related permits. Classification into simple, complex, and highly technical. Unified Building Permit Application Form and Unified Application Form for Certificates of Occupancy. Co-located one-stop shop (OBO, Treasury, Zoning, Assessor, BFP) and joint inspection teams. Application wizards must map field-for-field onto the unified forms, not onto a bespoke schema. Because inspections are joint, the app models one inspection event with multiple attending offices, not a queue of separate inspections.
RA 9514
Fire Code of the Philippines		 The BFP issues a Fire Safety Evaluation Clearance as a prerequisite to the building permit, and a Fire Safety Inspection Certificate before a newly constructed building may be occupied. FSEC is a tracked dependency of the building permit; FSIC is a tracked dependency of the Certificate of Occupancy. Both appear as first-class items with their own status, not as generic uploads buried in a document list.
RA 10173
Data Privacy Act of 2012		 Transparency, legitimate purpose, and proportionality. Informed consent before collection, with consent specific to purpose for sensitive personal information. Breach notification to the NPC and to affected data subjects. A privacy notice and explicit consent gate before the first document or photo is captured, stating purpose, retention, and recipients. In-app rights: access, correction, and deletion of uploaded documents. Camera, photo, and storage permission primers must state the purpose before the OS prompt.
RA 8792
Electronic Commerce Act of 2000		 Functional equivalence: electronic documents and signatures cannot be denied legal effect for being electronic, and electronic documents are admissible. Documents requiring notarisation are excluded. A permit downloaded in-app can be presented as a valid copy, so the app must offer a verifiable digital permit with permit number and a QR or reference for validation. Conversely, notarised items (application form, affidavits, Certificate of Completion, SPA) must be flagged in-app as requiring a wet-signed notarised original.
Zoning / Locational Clearance
issued by the City or Municipal Planning and Development Office / Zoning Administrator		 Certifies that the location and nature of the proposed development conform to the LGU's land-use rules. Secured prior to the building permit application. Modelled as a prerequisite with its own status on the building permit, with an explicit "already secured / not yet secured" branch at the start of the wizard so an applicant is not walked through nine steps only to be blocked.
LGU-issued supporting documents Barangay Clearance from the barangay with territorial jurisdiction; real property tax declarations from the City Assessor; RPT clearance from the City Treasurer; proof of ownership (Certified True Copy of TCT) from the Land Registration Authority. Each required document carries a "where to secure" line naming the issuing office, mirroring the LGU Citizen's Charter format. This is one of the highest-value, lowest-cost features in the entire app.
Deemed approval, handled carefully. RA 11032 provides for automatic approval where an office fails to act within the prescribed period, but implementing rules exclude matters implicating public health, safety, and national security — which is precisely the territory of building permits and occupancy. The app must therefore never assert to a user that their permit is automatically approved. It surfaces the lapsed pledge, the elapsed working days, and the available recourse, and leaves the legal conclusion to the LGU and ARTA.

3   Philippine platform benchmarks

What Philippine LGU e-permitting already does, and what eBPCO Mobile must therefore match or beat.

3.1   QC e-Services — Department of the Building Official

The most complete Philippine reference implementation for construction permits, and the closest analogue to eBPCO's scope. Its published Citizen's Charter for the Issuance of Construction Permits establishes a flow the mobile app should adopt wholesale:

QC e-Services mechanismWhat eBPCO Mobile must take from it
Confirmed online appointment as the first basic requirementAppointment booking is a first-class app feature, not an afterthought — with a reminder notification and the ability to reschedule.
Generated Checklist Online — the requirement list is generated per application type rather than published as one static listThe required-documents step must be derived from the answers already given, and the resulting checklist must be exportable and shareable with the applicant's professional.
Tagging of professionals — the design professional holds their own account and is tagged to the applicationModel the professional as a linked party with their own PRC and PTR credentials and validity dates, not as free text typed by the owner.
Letter of Instruction (LOI) issued to applicant and professional when documents are deficientThe LOI is a distinct, high-priority object in the app with its own screen, itemised deficiencies, a resubmission action, and notification to both parties.
Order of Payment (O.P.) delivered by email once the application is cleanThe O.P. is the trigger for the entire Payments tab. It arrives in-app with its line items intact, not as an opaque total.
Real-time status by application number, with statuses such as for evaluation, for payment, and releasedStatus lookup by application number must work, and the timeline must be granular enough to distinguish evaluation from assessment from release.
Multiple follow-up channels — portal, email, Viber community, Facebook pageGive a single authoritative in-app channel so the applicant never has to guess which one is current, plus the OBO's published contact details as fallback.

3.2   eBPLS — DICT / DILG

The national Electronic Business Permit and Licensing System, provided free to LGUs by the DICT under RA 11032, establishes the baseline expectation that Filipino applicants now carry into any LGU app: online submission of applications, digital payment, and issuance of electronic permits, licences, and clearances. eBPCO Mobile must meet all three or it is a regression against a system its users may already have used.

3.3   Pasig City OBO — Occupancy Permit checklist

Pasig's published checklist shows the true shape of an occupancy submission, and it is materially heavier than a generic upload list. It demands the Unified Application Form for Certificate of Occupancy; three notarised copies of the Certificate of Completion signed and sealed by the supervising architect or civil engineer — or by the contractor's Authorised Managing Officer where work was contracted; two sets of approved plans across architectural, civil/structural, and plumbing/sanitary; detailed bills of materials at actual cost; current PTR and valid PRC ID for each professional; photographs of the completed structure showing inside, front, sides, and rear; copies of the building and ancillary permits; the daily construction logbook; and separate signed and sealed completion forms for electrical, mechanical, and electronics works.

Design consequence. The Certificate of Occupancy flow is not a five-step form with an upload screen. It is a compilation task spanning months of construction. The app must therefore let the applicant accumulate occupancy evidence progressively — logbook entries, progress photographs, completion forms per discipline — from the moment the building permit is released, so that filing for occupancy is an act of assembly rather than a scramble.

Three states are currently collapsed and must not be. Revision Required, Assessed, and Ready for Release all demand action from the applicant, yet the first two are today folded into passive headlines. This flag drives the tab badge, the Home banner, and push priority.

Currency rule. The admin stores money in centavos as integers. Mobile must do the same and format only at the edge. Never hold a peso amount in a double: a fee schedule rendered with a floating-point rounding error is a defect an applicant will notice at the cashier.

Command N — Notification engine

BUILD SERVER Implement a single NotificationEvent model carrying id, type (one of N-01…N-24), priority, applicationId, payload, createdAt, readAt, and resolvedAt. Every notification in the app is one of these — there are no ad-hoc SnackBar-only events for anything on this list.

P1 events remain unresolved until the applicant completes the corresponding action, and an unresolved P1 keeps its banner and badge regardless of read state. Reading is not resolving. This distinction is the single most important behaviour in the notification system.

6   TAB 1 — Home

Command 1 — Home

Build Home as a triage surface, not a welcome screen. Its sole job is to put what the applicant owes at the top, what is moving in the middle, and what they can start at the bottom. If the applicant has an unresolved P1 notification, Home must be about that and nothing else above the fold.

The current dashboard shows a greeting, one active application, counters, quick actions, and recent notifications. Keep the structure; invert the priority so that action items outrank decoration, and replace every mock counter with a real projection.

6.1   Composition, in render order

6.3   Acceptance criteria

  • An unresolved P1 always renders above the fold on a 360×640 dp viewport.
  • The working-day countdown excludes Saturdays, Sundays, and Philippine regular and special non-working holidays, and is computed from an injectable calendar service so it is testable.
  • Every counter navigates to Applications pre-filtered to exactly the set it counted.
  • Blocks render independently; one failing repository cannot blank the tab.
  • Widget test drives: no-business, business-without-application, one-in-flight, one-LOI-outstanding, and overdue-payment states.

7   TAB 2 — Applications

Command 2 — Applications

Build Applications as the full lifecycle record of everything the applicant has filed or started, plus the catalog for starting more. The sixteen permit wizards already exist and are substantially complete — do not rebuild them. Build the layer around them: the list, the detail, the timeline, the deficiency loop, the inspection record, and the permit itself.

The single largest gap in the app today is that an application effectively ends at submission. Everything after submission — evaluation stages, letters of instruction, revisions, inspections, approval, permit generation, release — currently has no home. That is what this command builds.

7.1   Screens

7.3   Behaviour rules

  • The applicant never changes status. Every action the app offers is a submission into the admin's queue — resubmit after an LOI, submit proof of payment, confirm an appointment. Status changes arrive from the server only.
  • Remarks are instructions. Wherever the admin recorded remarks on a Revision Required or Rejected result, render them verbatim and prominently. Never summarise, truncate behind a "more" link, or paraphrase an evaluator.
  • Drafts are durable. A draft survives app restart, is listed with its completion percentage, and can be resumed at the exact step it was left on. This already works — preserve it.
  • Ancillary permits link to their parent. An electrical, mechanical, sanitary, or electronics permit filed against an existing building permit shows that parent in its header and in the parent's detail.

7.4   Acceptance criteria

  • Every one of the 19 lifecycleStatus values renders with a correct headline, sub-line, and timeline entry — verified by a table-driven widget test over all 19.
  • An application with an open LOI cannot reach a state where the applicant sees no route to resolve it.
  • The timeline renders a revision loop without duplicating or reordering earlier steps.
  • The digital permit is readable and shareable with no network connection once downloaded.
  • All 16 wizards still pass their existing widget tests after the surrounding layer is added.

8   TAB 3 — Payments

Command 3 — Payments

Build Payments around the Order of Payment as the only legitimate source of an amount due. The tab exists to do four things: show what is owed and why, let the applicant pay or record a payment, prove the payment was made, and produce the official receipt afterwards.

The app must never display an amount the LGU has not assessed. Where no assessment exists, the correct state is Not Yet Available with an explanation of what triggers assessment — not a zero, not an estimate, and not a blank.

8.1   Screens

8.2   State machine

Not Yet Available  ──(admin assesses; lifecycleStatus → Assessed)──▶  Due Now
Due Now            ──(applicant submits reference + proof)────────▶  Pending Verification
Due Now            ──(due date passes, unpaid)────────────────────▶  Overdue
Pending Verification ──(Treasury verifies)────────────────────────▶  Paid
Pending Verification ──(rejected: wrong amount/reference)─────────▶  Due Now  + P1 notice
Overdue            ──(applicant pays)─────────────────────────────▶  Pending Verification
Do not build a payment gateway into this release. The admin domain accepts exactly two methods — Bank Transfer and Onsite — and payment is verified by the Treasurer's Office rather than captured by the app. Present only those two. E-wallet and over-the-counter channels used elsewhere in Philippine LGU practice may be added later, but only once the LGU has formally accepted them; offering an unaccepted channel would strand an applicant's money.

8.3   Acceptance criteria

  • All monetary values are held as integer centavos end to end and formatted only for display, as PHP 12,345.67.
  • The sum of rendered line items always equals the rendered total; a mismatch is a hard error state, never a silently rounded figure.
  • No screen can display an amount when assessedAmountCentavos is null.
  • Proof of payment cannot be submitted without both a reference number and an attachment.
  • Overdue state is reachable and tested, and raises N-11.

9   TAB 4 — Notifications

Command 4 — Notifications

Rebuild Notifications from a passive list into a work queue. Its organising principle is not chronology but resolution: things that still require the applicant to act sit above things that are merely news, regardless of age.

Implement the full catalog in Section 5. Every entry deep-links to the exact screen where the thing can be dealt with — never to a tab root, and never to a dead end.

9.1   Structure

SectionContents and behaviour
Needs your actionAll unresolved P1 events, ordered by urgency then age. Each carries its primary action inline. An item leaves this section only when its underlying condition is resolved — reading it does not remove it.
UpdatesP2 progress events, reverse-chronological, grouped by day with Today and Yesterday headers.
EarlierP3 ambient events and everything already read and resolved. Collapsed by default beyond 30 days.
FiltersBy application, by business, and unread-only. Mark all as read applies to read state only and never clears an unresolved action.

9.2   Delivery rules

  • Push is earned, not assumed. P1 always pushes. P2 pushes unless the applicant has muted that category. P3 never pushes.
  • Quiet hours. No push between 21:00 and 07:00 Philippine Standard Time except for a P1 whose deadline falls within the following 24 hours.
  • Per-category preferences live in Profile and must genuinely gate delivery. A preferences screen that does not change behaviour is a defect — this is the current state and must be corrected.
  • Badging on the tab shows the count of unresolved P1s, not unread count. This is deliberate: the number should mean "things you must do", not "things you have not looked at".
  • Every notification carries its application reference in the body text, because an applicant with several permits in flight cannot otherwise tell which one is being discussed.

9.3   Acceptance criteria

  • Each of N-01 through N-24 is produced, rendered, and deep-links to a live destination — covered by one table-driven test.
  • Marking all as read leaves every unresolved P1 in Needs your action, with the tab badge unchanged.
  • Muting a category suppresses its push and continues to record the feed entry.
  • Deep links resolve correctly from a cold start, not only when the app is already running.

10   TAB 5 — Profile

Command 5 — Profile

Build Profile as the identity, evidence, and compliance tab. It holds who the applicant is, the businesses and properties they file under, the design professionals tagged to their applications, the reusable document vault, and the privacy controls the Data Privacy Act requires. Settings are the least important thing here and should not dominate it.

The document vault is the highest-leverage feature in this tab. An applicant who has already uploaded their PRC ID, TCT, and barangay clearance should never be asked to find them again for the next permit.

10.1   Sections

SectionStateContent
IdentityEXTENDName, mobile, email, photo, and verification state. Mobile and email must be individually verified because both carry official correspondence.
Businesses & propertiesEXTENDEach business or property owner profile with its address, ownership evidence, and the applications filed under it. Applications are always filed under one of these, never against a bare user.
Design professionalsBUILDArchitects and engineers tagged to the applicant's projects, each with PRC number and validity, PTR number, date and place of issue, discipline, and their PRC ID and PTR images. Expiry drives N-22. Directly mirrors the tagging-of-professionals model used by QC e-Services.
Authorised representativesBUILDPeople permitted to file or claim on the applicant's behalf, each with their notarised Special Power of Attorney and valid ID on file. Feeds the Authorized Representative release method.
My DocumentsKEEPAlready built — camera, gallery, and file import; categorisation; in-app PDF and image preview; reuse into any permit upload slot. Add expiry dates for time-bound documents and a validity warning.
Privacy & dataBUILDThe privacy notice, the consents given with their dates, and working controls to access, correct, export, and delete personal data — the Data Privacy Act rights made operational rather than described.
SecurityEXTENDChange password, biometric unlock, active sessions, and sign out. See the warning below.
Help & charterEXTENDThe LGU's Citizen's Charter entry for each permit service — classification, pledged processing time, fees, and requirements — plus OBO contact details, office hours, and the ARTA complaint route.
SettingsEXTENDNotification preferences that genuinely gate delivery. Language: either implement localisation for the listed Philippine languages or reduce the picker to what is actually supported.
Security defect to correct before any pilot. The app currently stores credentials in plain text in SharedPreferences to simulate login. Under the Data Privacy Act this is indefensible once real applicant data is present. Replace with a token-based session, store tokens in the platform keychain or keystore via secure storage, and never persist a password on the device. Treat this as blocking for any release that touches real personal data.
Language picker. The screen lists twenty Philippine languages but changes nothing — it only moves a radio button. Presenting a choice that does not function is worse than presenting no choice. Either wire localisation properly, starting with English and Filipino, or reduce the list to the languages actually supported and mark the rest as coming soon.

10.2   Acceptance criteria

  • No password is written to disk in any form; a static analysis check enforces this.
  • A document in the vault can be attached to a permit upload slot in three taps or fewer.
  • Every notification preference toggle demonstrably changes delivery, proven by test.
  • Data deletion removes the local file from app storage as well as the record, and says what it cannot delete server-side and why.
  • Every permit type in the catalog has a reachable Citizen's Charter entry stating its classification and pledged processing time.

11   Cross-cutting commands

These cut across all five tabs. Each is owned by no single tab and must be built once, centrally.

Command X1 — Working-day and service-pledge engine

Implement a single ServicePledgeService that, given an application's classification and date of filing, returns the pledged completion date, working days elapsed, working days remaining, and whether the pledge has lapsed. It must exclude weekends and Philippine regular and special non-working holidays from an injectable calendar, so tests are deterministic and the holiday list can be updated yearly without touching logic. Every countdown in the app reads from this one service. Nothing computes dates locally.

Command X2 — Document pipeline and vault

Consolidate document handling on the existing attach-document sheet — camera, gallery, system file picker, and My Documents — for every upload slot in the app. Eleven of the sixteen wizards still fabricate mock documents; migrate them all, and add debugAttachDocumentOverride to each wizard's test file in the same change.

Every stored document carries a category, an optional expiry date, its source, and a server-side status of Pending, Approved, Rejected, or Missing. A document rejected by an evaluator displays its rejection remark wherever it appears.

Command X3 — Privacy, consent, and permissions

Gate the first capture of any personal document behind a privacy notice stating purpose, retention, and who receives the data, with explicit recorded consent and its timestamp. Precede every OS permission prompt with a primer explaining the purpose in plain language — the app already has this pattern for camera, photos, and file access; extend it consistently. Provide working access, correction, export, and deletion controls in Profile.

Command X4 — Accessibility and low-end device performance

Every interactive target at least 48×48 dp — already an app constant, apply it without exception. All content legible at 200% text scale with no clipping; the nine-step wizards are the highest risk and must be tested at that scale. Semantic labels on every icon-only control. Contrast at WCAG AA against the existing palette. Target a mid-range Android device on a throttled connection as the reference, not a simulator on a desktop.

Command X5 — Offline and sync

Drafts, the document vault, and downloaded permits are fully available offline. Every remote-backed surface carries an "as of {timestamp}" stamp when serving cached data. Submissions attempted offline queue with an explicit pending-submission state and retry; they never fail silently and never double-submit on retry.

Content
